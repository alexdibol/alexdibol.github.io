AI-Enhanced Institutional Knowledge

From protected knowledge vaults to governed cognitive evolution.

This repository presents an end-to-end framework for building AI-enhanced institutional knowledge: a governed system through which an organization can connect its protected knowledge resources to large language models, generate new candidate knowledge, validate and authorize what enters institutional memory, and study how that memory evolves over time.

The central proposition is that the strategic unit of organizational AI is not the model alone. It is the complete institutional system that combines human judgment, structured and unstructured organizational knowledge, generative and reasoning models, and governed, AI-enhanced institutional memory.

The architecture is designed to help corporations integrate AI into their processes while preserving control over proprietary information. A governed context-construction layer retrieves the minimum eligible evidence for a declared purpose and access profile. Model outputs return as candidate knowledge and must pass deterministic controls, provenance checks, policy rules, and, where required, human approval before they can become canonical institutional knowledge.

Cybersecurity in the Age of Autonomous AI

From foundational cyber risk to governed defense against agentic threats.

A professional, book-supported course for financial practitioners, Master of Finance students, executives, and board members who must understand how autonomous AI changes the speed, scale, persistence, and economics of cyber risk.

The learning system combines a ten-chapter book, ten governance-first companion notebooks, a dedicated Safe Lab research paper, and ten progressively sophisticated Safe Lab notebooks. The sequence moves from protecting one synthetic financial record to prompt injection, adaptive red-team cognition, red–blue interaction, agent mutation, poisoned institutional memory, false consensus, state equivocation, deterministic containment, and causal recovery.

Every laboratory is synthetic, bounded, and pedagogical. The central governance principle is that operational intelligence must remain separate from operational authority: models and agents may analyze and propose, while independent controls, evidence, and accountable human decision rights govern consequential action.
