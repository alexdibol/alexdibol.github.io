Purpose and method

Next-generation education, designed to stay current

Traditional courses are episodic. A frontier-facing curriculum must be living: stable where principles endure, revised where architectures change, and exploratory where important new capabilities appear. Classroom teaching, including graduate-level instruction, provides an ongoing opportunity to test explanations and laboratories; each new iteration informs the next.

01 · Enduring foundations

Preserve economic reasoning, statistical discipline, market mechanisms, robust evaluation, controls and professional accountability. Revise when evidence—not fashion—requires it.

02 · Evolving architectures

Revisit courses and experiments as machine learning gives way to conversational models, tool-using agents, coordinated systems and more adaptive architectures.

03 · Frontier exploration

Investigate consequential developments early, distinguish promise from demonstrated capability, and translate experiments into financial use cases with explicit uncertainty.

Guiding progression: Understand the mechanisms and limitations → Govern decisions, risks and authority → Create and implement useful applications. Governance remains embedded throughout design, testing and operation.
One knowledge base · Three educational interfaces

Built for the decisions people actually face

The same technological advance matters differently to a board director, a professional managing a financial workflow, and a graduate student preparing to enter the field. The platform adapts depth, cases and expected outcomes—not the underlying intellectual standards.

Continuous research-to-teaching cycle

Explore → Experiment → Distill → Teach → Update

The author identifies questions at the intersection of emerging AI and financial practice, uses AI to accelerate exploration and prototyping, tests mechanisms through executable laboratories, and curates the lessons that professionals need. The objective is not to outsource judgment to AI, but to use AI to investigate better questions and expose the limits of proposed answers.

Research as reconnaissance

Scan new models, architectures and practices; ask which advances materially change a financial task, decision or institutional control.

Experimental education

Connect monographs, lecture decks, podcasts and runnable Colab notebooks. Make assumptions, intermediate steps, failure modes and governance visible.

Living releases

Move toward periodic frontier editions that explicitly state what remains valid, what requires revision, and what deserves fresh investigation. This is the evolving delivery model, not a claim of an existing paid service.

Research and teaching discipline: Emerging capabilities are examined through financial problems, executable experiments and critical review. AI-assisted exploration accelerates discovery, while verification, governance and professional judgment remain essential.
